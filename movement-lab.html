Test procedure

Keep phone stationary. Align card edges to white outline. Mark reference once. Hold it still, nudge it slightly and return it, then slowly slide the top card off. Try adding another card on top. Count one candidate per actual swap. A hand, large repositioning of the same card, or a different textured surface can cause false candidates. Keep the phone fixed.
